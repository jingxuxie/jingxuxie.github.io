Hi, welcome to my homepage!

I am a physicist, AI/quant researcher, and research engineer. I completed my Ph.D. at the [University of California, Berkeley](https://physics.berkeley.edu/) and [Lawrence Berkeley National Laboratory](https://www.lbl.gov/), where I worked with [Prof. Feng Wang](https://physics.berkeley.edu/research-faculty/ultrafast-nano-optics-group) on quantum materials, light-matter interactions, and electrical transport in low-dimensional systems.

My work combines scientific modeling, machine learning, computer vision, automated instrumentation, and large-scale data analysis. I have built tools for optical microscopy and 2D-material identification, automated laser alignment and wafer scribing, quantitative forecasting, portfolio optimization, and retrieval-augmented generation for scientific and engineering workflows.

I am especially interested in AI systems that can reason, use tools, interact with the physical world, and accelerate scientific discovery. More broadly, I enjoy working at the boundary between deep research and practical engineering: turning complex physical, computational, or data-driven problems into working systems.

## Selected projects

- **Automated 2D Materials Optical Identification** — Computer-vision and transfer-matrix-based software for identifying 2D material thickness from optical microscope images.
- **Laser Auto-Alignment for Cryogenic Measurements** — Image-based feedback control system that corrects laser drift using camera-derived intensity, spot size, and position signals.
- **Silicon Wafer Auto-Scriber** — Camera-guided alignment and motor-control system for cutting large silicon wafers into smaller dies.
- **Scientific RAG System** — Retrieval-augmented generation system for laboratory manuals, engineering logs, and technical emails.
- **Quantitative Forecasting and Optimization** — Predictive modeling and portfolio optimization for quantitative research workflows.
- **Predictive Coding for Efficient AI Hardware** — Research on local learning algorithms and analog hardware constraints for energy-efficient neural computation.
