This page highlights selected projects that connect my background in physics, machine learning, quantitative research, scientific software, and experimental automation.

## Automated 2D Materials Optical Identification

I developed software for automated identification of two-dimensional material flakes from optical microscope images. The system combines image processing with transfer-matrix optical modeling to estimate material thickness from optical contrast, helping researchers screen candidate flakes more efficiently.

**Keywords:** computer vision, scientific imaging, transfer-matrix modeling, microscopy, Python

## Laser Auto-Alignment for Cryogenic Optical Measurements

In cryogenic optical/electrical measurements, thermal drift can gradually move the laser away from the target sample. I built an image-based feedback-control system that analyzes camera images of the laser spot, including intensity, width, and position, then adjusts mirrors or piezo stages to keep the laser focused near the sample center.

**Keywords:** feedback control, computer vision, optical alignment, motorized stages, experimental automation

## Silicon Wafer Auto-Scriber

I built a camera-guided wafer-alignment and scribing system for cutting large silicon wafers into smaller dies. The system detects wafer position and rotation, aligns the crystal orientation using rotational and translational stages, and coordinates the motion path of a diamond scriber.

**Keywords:** robotics-style automation, machine vision, motion control, wafer handling, prototyping

## Scientific RAG System

I am building a retrieval-augmented generation system for laboratory manuals, engineering logs, and technical emails. The goal is to make scattered scientific and engineering knowledge searchable, auditable, and useful for troubleshooting real experimental systems.

**Keywords:** retrieval-augmented generation, LLMs, embeddings, search, scientific knowledge management

## Quantitative Forecasting and Optimization

I have worked on predictive modeling and optimization problems in quantitative research, including forecast-realization modeling, time-series analysis, and portfolio optimization under risk and transaction-cost constraints.

**Keywords:** quantitative research, time-series modeling, portfolio optimization, Bayesian optimization, Python

## Predictive Coding for Efficient AI Hardware

I have explored predictive-coding and local-learning algorithms as possible alternatives to standard backpropagation for energy-efficient neural computation, especially under analog hardware constraints such as quantization, noise, and device nonlinearity.

**Keywords:** predictive coding, local learning, efficient AI, analog hardware, JAX/PyTorch
